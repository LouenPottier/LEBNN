Code and data from the LEBNN article (JMPS 2025) 
Latent-Energy-Based NNs: An interpretable Neural Network architecture for model-order reduction of nonlinear statics in solid mechanics
L. Pottier, A. Thorin, F. Chinesta
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022509624004198

## Related work

The code was adapted and extended to nonlinear dynamics in the Latent-Lagrangian Neural Networks (LLNN):

- A. K. Agrawal, A. Thorin, *Latent-Lagrangian neural networks for reduced order modeling of non-autonomous nonlinear dynamical systems*, JMPS (2026). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmps.2026.106633
- A. K. Agrawal, L. Pottier, A. Thorin, *Latent-Lagrangian Neural Networks for Model-Order Reduction of Nonlinear Dynamical Systems*, MORTech 2025. https://louenpottier.github.io/pdf/MORtech2025_LLNN.pdf

The data were reused in:

- L. Pottier, L. Lesueur, A. Thorin, *Partially Linear Autoencoders for Manifold Learning and Dimensionality Reduction*, arXiv (2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29867
